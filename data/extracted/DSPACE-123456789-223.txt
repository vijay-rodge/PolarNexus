AMATEUR RADIO OPERATION DURING THE THIRD INDIAN ANTARCTIC EXPEDITION 1983 - 84
Phone-patch traffic from various U. S. Antarctic Bases and operation of many Antarctic region
stations were monitored to study their operating habits.
Aerial Survey of Wholthot Mountain was done in Ml 8 helicopter to study VMF propagation
in the area from the point of view of installing unmanned VHF repeater at a suitable location.
Radio traffic of the expedition on both HF and VHF was monitored to study the effectiveness
of radio communication planning and performance of various radio equipments.
In all amateur Radio contacts, 100 watts R F power inverted-V (dipole) and Qubical Quad
antenna were used.
